Duo synergies

2026 pro meta vs. our pools

Play it

  • Jarvan IV: most picked at MSI 2026 (19 games). Smette, ShaiTing, Heino.
  • Ezreal: 3rd most picked at MSI (18). EP Theyron's safest ADC.
  • Varus: most banned all spring (700+ bans). EP Theyron, 5-1 in 2026.
  • Xin Zhao: in 4 of 6 LEC playoff teams' top 5. Smette, Heino.

Be careful

  • Sion: won 1 of 10 MSI games. Prefer Cho'Gath.
  • Jhin: 13% win rate at MSI. Prefer Varus in pick comps.
  • Galio: 17% at MSI. Lissandra is the better engage mid.

Ban or respect

  • MSI most banned: Poppy, Vi, Jayce, Orianna, Akali.
  • Best MSI win rates: Dr. Mundo, Lucian, Milio (HennenG has it), Trundle.

Sources: rft.gg MSI 2026, pem.lol, rdy.gg LEC Spring 2026, Hotspawn.

Match day

Before

  • Opponent scouted, bans agreed
  • 2–3 comps from above picked, first choice per role
  • Everyone in voice 15 minutes early, client restarted, rune pages saved
  • Current Prime League rulebook checked (format, check-in, side choice)

In game

  • One shotcaller for objectives and fights; everyone else gives info
  • Short calls: "Flash mid down", "Jungle seen bot", "Drake in 60"
  • Objectives: push lanes, set vision, then start. Never without prio.
  • No flaming. Mistakes get discussed after the game.

After

  • 5-minute review: when did the game turn, and why?
  • Update the draft pool sheet if someone found a new comfort pick
